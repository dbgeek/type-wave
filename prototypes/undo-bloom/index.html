Undo bloom — confirm & refuse HUD treatments

The Undo-last-Insertion hotkey (⌃⌘⌫) needs a HUD cue with two outcomes: a confirmed deletion and a refused one. Unlike the amber degraded pulse, at Undo time nothing is recording or processing — the pill is hidden, so a cue must bloom up from nothing, then fade. Trigger each treatment below and pick a direction. The pill is the real 300×22 geometry, shown at 2.4×, on the real 0.7× motion timings.

What this ticket must lock

  • Hue / motion — which treatment, and the exact confirm vs refuse look, distinct from each other and non-colliding with the amber pulse (R).
  • Pill content — single mark (A/C) or reused dots (B), given nothing is in flight at Undo time.
  • ADR-0002 — if the pick uses an accent (A or B), it is the second sanctioned accent exception → warrants an ADR amendment (extend ADR-0004 or a new ADR) for the spec to cite. Pick C and ADR-0002 stays as-is.
